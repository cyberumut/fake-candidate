FAKE CANDIDATE

A pixel-art political satire brawler for iPhone & iPad

Eternal President Vic Tory has won every election for 40 years with 99.9%. The opposition's last hope: ZERO, a deepfake candidate built in a basement by retired hacker Auntie Nermin.

Hit the streets at night, debunk lies, and convince the regime's trolls, pollsters and riot bots across 200 levels and 5 districts. Chain combo moves like FACT CHECK, HEADLINE SLAM and MIC DROP, face 5 bosses, and win the vote.

FAQ

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I get an extra life?
After a defeat you can watch an optional rewarded video (limited per day).

How do I limit ad tracking?
iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
