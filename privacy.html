Fake Candidate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Fake Candidate ("the game") is a single-player mobile game. This policy explains what data the game and its third-party advertising partner handle.

1. Data the game itself stores

The game stores your progress (unlocked levels, stars, in-game donations, upgrades, settings) only on your device. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and it does not send your progress to any server we operate. Deleting the app deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad after a defeat, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for an extra life. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and IDFV;
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model, OS version, language;
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, video completion) and diagnostic/crash data from the ads SDK.

On iOS you are asked through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt whether ads may use your advertising identifier. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchase (RevenueCat)

The game offers one optional in-app purchase, "Remove Ads". Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To validate the purchase and restore it on your devices, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app (product, date, store transaction ID) and basic device information (such as OS version and country). This data is used only to provide the purchase, not for advertising or tracking.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. Your choices

  • Change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings.
  • Uninstall the game to remove all locally stored data. Purchases stay linked to your Apple Account and can be restored with RESTORE in the pause menu.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: open an issue on our support page.
